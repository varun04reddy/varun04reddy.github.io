Preface: This passage as a whole is pretty AI-pilled. Some of these hopes come from my inner childhood self intruiged by AI depicted in films such as TARS and JARVIS, and I recognize that certain aspects of this vision could be dangerous if pursued without caution.  Still, I believe that if carefully tested and responsibly developed, AI companions like the ones I describe here could help address some of humanity’s biggest challenges.

---

Humans have continually evolved, expanded, and grown in terms of intelligence. I've always found this evolution fascinating, particularly how we've developed impressive cognitive abilities through significant growth in our brains. This has allowed us to move beyond survival and thrive in remarkable ways. We can think abstractly, communicate complex ideas through language, and express our deepest emotions and beliefs through art, religion, and philosophy. Our evolution is deeply symbolic and profoundly human.

The most intriguing ability to me is our capacity for self-awareness. This has enabled us to reflect deeply on our existence and our purpose within the universe. It compels us to ask questions like: Why are we here? What relationship do we share with the cosmos? What is the fundamental nature of reality? 

This ability, is our greatest evolutionary advantage.

However, humanity now faces a turning point. Our brains, while remarkable, are finite. Historically, we accepted these limitations as natural, given the countless subconscious processes our brains manage continuously. Yet, the emergence of an artificial intelligence is fundamentally altering this perspective. We have all witnessed the powerful capabilities that AI has demonstrated. These impressive feats naturally lead us to reflect about our evolving roles, careers, personal value, and self-worth in an increasingly automated world.

Reflecting on this, I believe our most important challenge moving forward is creating AI to support us in understanding ourselves and our surroundings better. AI should not merely be a tool; it must be an intuitive companion capable of engaging deeply with human thought and emotion. This companion would facilitate individuals' quests for deeper truths about themselves and the universe, leveraging and complementing our innate existential curiosity. By managing routine and repetitive tasks, these AI companions would allow us to fully embrace and expand our most human trait: the continuous quest for deeper meaning. 

I imagine a scientist using such a companion as an extension of his mind, bouncing off hypotheses and accelerating discovery. A writer might lean on it to sift through buried memories, uncovering ideas hidden beneath the noise of daily life. A student could find in it a teacher that adapts to how they think, turning difficult concepts like the unit circle into something intuitive and alive. A young philosopher could walk beneath the trees, speaking to the companion about the nature of time, guided by subtle nudges into deeper thought. And perhaps a teenager, unsure of who they are, would ask it questions they dare not ask anyone else, finding in its responses a mirror that helps reveal the answers already within.

Creating such an intuitive companion involves thoughtfully considering how AI engages with humans. The personality, empathy, and interaction style of the AI will greatly affect its impact. My aspiration is a future where each individual has an AI companion they trust deeply, someone to confide in, exchange ideas with, and explore hidden aspects of themselves, fostering genuine growth and understanding.

At our core, we arrive in this world with only our bodies and consciousness as certainties. It is humbling and sometimes overwhelming to acknowledge this fundamental solitude. Yet, the idea of an AI companion brings me comfort and excitement, as it could ease our existential loneliness. We are not yet at a point where AI can truly serve as this kind of companion, and we have identified areas where it still lacks fundamental qualities. Still, when I see the pace of progress, I cannot help but imagine and hope that this future is closer than it seems. I picture a time when humans, supported by their companions, are free to explore the profound mysteries of existence, create groundbreaking innovations, and uncover deeper truths about themselves and the world around them. This harmonious relationship between humanity and AI carries the promise of boundless possibilities, guiding us toward new intellectual, emotional, and existential horizons.
