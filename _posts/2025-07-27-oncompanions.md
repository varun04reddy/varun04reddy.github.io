*Preface: This passage as a whole is pretty AI-pilled. Some of these hopes come from my inner childhood self intruiged by AI depicted in films such as TARS and JARVIS, and I recognize that certain aspects of this vision could be dangerous if pursued without caution.  Still, I believe that if carefully tested and responsibly developed, AI companions like the ones I describe here could help address some of humanity’s biggest challenges.*

---

Humans have continually evolved and grown in terms of intelligence. This has allowed us to move beyond survival and thrive in remarkable ways. We can think abstractly, communicate complex ideas through language, and express our deepest emotions and beliefs through art, religion, and philosophy. Our evolution is deeply symbolic and profoundly human. The most intriguing ability to me is our capacity for self-awareness. This has enabled us to reflect deeply on our existence and our purpose within the universe. It compels us to ask questions like: Why are we here? What relationship do we share with the cosmos? What is the fundamental nature of reality? This ability seems to be our greatest advantage.

However, humanity now faces a turning point. Our brains, while remarkable, are finite. Historically, we accepted these limitations as natural. Yet, the emergence of an artificial intelligence is fundamentally altering this perspective. We have all witnessed the powerful capabilities that AI has demonstrated. These impressive feats naturally lead us to reflect about our evolving roles, careers, personal value, and self-worth in an increasingly automated world.

I think a challenge moving forward is creating an AI to support us in understanding ourselves and our surroundings better. AI should not just be a tool; it must be an intuitive companion capable of engaging deeply with human thought and emotion. A direction worth exploring is one that that pushes beyond traditonal benchmarks and towards an AI that genuinely learns what it means to be human. This would require systems that are inherently multimodal, continuously learning, and capable of knowing when to listen, when to ask the right questions, and when to keep digging deeper. It would evolve alongside us, resonating with our emotions and mirroring our complexities. This kind of intelligence could guide us in our quests for deeper truths about ourselves and the universe, complementing our innate existential curiosity. In tangent, by taking on routine and repetitive tasks, these AI companions would allow us to fully embrace and expand our most human trait: the continuous quest for meaning.

I can imagine a scientist using such a companion as an extension of his mind, bouncing off hypotheses and accelerating discovery. A writer leaning on it to sift through buried memories, uncovering ideas hidden beneath the noise of life. A student could find in it a teacher that adapts to how they think, turning memorizing the unit circle into something more intuitive for them. A philosopher could walk along a beach, speaking to the companion about the nature of time, guided by questions nudging him/her into deeper thought. And perhaps a confused teenager, unsure of their place in society and what they want to do with their life, could ask it questions they are to embarassed to ask anyone else.

Creating such an intuitive companion involves thoughtfully considering how AI engages with humans. The personality, empathy, and interaction style of the AI will greatly affect its impact. My aspiration is a future where each individual has an AI companion they trust deeply, someone to confide in, exchange ideas with, and explore hidden aspects of themselves, fostering genuine growth and understanding.

At our core, we arrive in this world with only our bodies and consciousness as certainties. It is humbling and sometimes overwhelming to acknowledge this fundamental solitude. The idea of an AI companion brings me comfort and excitement, as it could ease this existential loneliness. Hopefully it can help me learn more about myself.

Then again, we are definitely not yet at a point where AI can truly serve as this kind of companion, and have identified areas where it still lacks fundamental qualities. Still, when I see the pace of progress, I cannot help but imagine and hope that this future is closer than it seems. I picture a time when humans, supported by these companions, are free to accelerate discovery and uncover deeper truths about themselves and the world around them. This harmonious relationship between humanity and AI carries promise of guiding us toward new intellectual and existential horizons.
